10. ![Pasted image 20260724075936.png](/img/user/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260724075936.png)

11. Then make sure to select the correct regional account that we need to quote out from. RFDC IMP002, CLDC IMP003, PNP IMP004
12. ![Pasted image 20260724080423.png](/img/user/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260724080423.png)
13. Then under the selection "Place case in queue" select "Sales and Service Centre"![Pasted image 20260724080654.png](/img/user/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260724080654.png)
14. Under quote required select "Yes"![Pasted image 20260724080736.png](/img/user/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260724080736.png)
15. Under fitment required select "No"![Pasted image 20260804153822.png](/img/user/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260804153822.png)
16. In the quote information section please specify qty, size, make, pattern, warehouse, price.  
17. In the special instructions please quote the reference numbers from the subject in the email request received, also any vehicle registration number if received.
18. PO Supplied you can leave empty.
19. You don't need to upload any files.
20. After clicking next you will get a case number.
21. You will then receive an quotation via email from noreply@salesforce.com, 
22. Forward the quotation the georgem@att-tyres.co.za for approval on the back of the original email request. 
23. KAM will forward the approved quotation the the person who initiated the request.
24. The Site Supervisor then needs to request a PO from the client.
25. The client will then forward the PO the dpwpurchaseorder@att-tyres.co.za
26. The store man at Boron will then forward the PO to the Sales and Service Centre for them to invoice out.
27. Delivery will then take place.
28. After the quotation is requested please make sure to update the excel schedule.
